---

title: "SAAKSHI"
description: "A brief poem on sea and wind, earth and sky."
pubDate: 2021-03-26
-------------------

Merged in the blue

Dancing along the waves

Her eyes filled with ocean

She touches the earth time and again.

Rejoicing under the sun

Filled with warmth abundant

He sways away with the blows of wind

Caressing the leaves to and fro.
