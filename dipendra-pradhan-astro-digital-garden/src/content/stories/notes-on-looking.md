A photograph is sometimes less about what was seen than about the patience that made seeing possible.

The Fujifilm X-S10 is a small machine, but it changes the walk. It asks the hand to notice before the mind has finished naming. A balcony, a doorway, a shoulder of light on a wall: each becomes an invitation to pause.

## What Remains

The best images often keep some privacy. They do not explain everything. They leave room for weather, silence, distance, and the viewer's own memory.

That is the spirit this archive is meant to hold: not a display case, but a notebook that keeps opening.
